Join ELIXIR Ireland

How an Irish research institute becomes a member of the Node

Why join

ELIXIR Ireland is a network of research institutes that together form the Irish Node of ELIXIR. Membership is free: Research Ireland pays Ireland's ELIXIR fee, which covers any number of institutes.

  • Take part in ELIXIR Europe. Membership lets your researchers formally join ELIXIR Platforms, Communities and Focus Groups, and apply for ELIXIR calls such as Implementation Studies and Commissioned Services.
  • National coordination. The Node brokers involvement in international projects, supports travel and staff exchange, and connects you to more than 240 research organisations across Europe.
  • Research infrastructure strategy. ELIXIR is a key stakeholder in the European Open Science Cloud (EOSC). Membership gives a direct route into EOSC and national work such as EOSC Ireland.
  • Visibility. Your institute is listed as a member, and your people are visible in national and European infrastructure work.

How to join

  1. Express interest

    A group leader (PI) at the institute contacts the Head of Node and Node Coordinator. Fill in the form below and your email app opens with the message ready to send.

  2. Management Board approval

    We add your application to the agenda of the next Management Board meeting. The Board meets at least once a quarter, and its members vote on each new institute.

  3. Sign the national collaboration agreement

    After approval, the ELIXIR Ireland national collaboration agreement is updated to include your institute and signed by all member institutes. Email elixir@ul.ie for a copy.

    The agreement must be signed by someone with authority to represent your institute. At current members this is one of:

    • Director of Financial Management
    • Vice President for Research and Innovation, or Vice President Research
    • Associate Director, Innovation Office
    • Director of Research

    Your institute also names four contacts:

    • Project manager: the PI leading the application
    • Legal: reviews and approves the agreement update
    • Post-award finance and administration
    • Intellectual property and confidentiality

  4. ELIXIR Europe update

    Once everyone has signed, ELIXIR Ireland updates its agreement with ELIXIR Europe to add your institute. You don't need to do anything for this step.

Timing: new institutes are added through an update to the national agreement, which all members sign. Updates are grouped into an annual cycle, so the time to join depends on where you apply in that cycle. We will confirm dates when you get in touch.

Institutes in Northern Ireland

Institutes in Northern Ireland join ELIXIR through ELIXIR-UK, the UK Node. Membership is free and has its own application process. We work closely with ELIXIR-UK and welcome joint work across the island.
