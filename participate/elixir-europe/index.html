Connect to ELIXIR Europe

Your institute's membership of ELIXIR Ireland lets you join ELIXIR's European groups. Here is how to get set up.

Do the national steps first if you haven't. Your ticks are saved in this browser only.

0 of 6 done

  1. Create your LS Login account

    LS Login (Life Science Login) is the single sign-on for ELIXIR. Register with your institution's login so your account is linked to your institute. You'll choose your organisation, fill in a short form, accept the usage policy and confirm your email.

    Register for LS Login Registration guide

    Problems signing up: support@aai.lifescience-ri.eu

  2. Join your Platforms, Communities and Focus Groups

    This is how you get onto the ELIXIR intranet. Open a group's page and press Join at the bottom. You sign in with LS Login, and you're added to that group's mailing list and the intranet. Join as many groups as you like.

    • Platforms build and run ELIXIR's core services. Members only.
    • Communities bring together experts in a life science area. Most meet in person once a year.
    • Focus Groups are time-limited groups working on one topic.
    Browse the groups

  3. Log in to the ELIXIR intranet

    Once you've joined a group, log in with LS Login. The intranet has the Handbook of Operations, ELIXIR templates and logos, All Hands registration, and details of travel grants and staff exchange. You can manage your groups there too.

    Log in to the intranet Manage my groups

  4. Join ELIXIR Slack

    Most groups chat day to day on the ELIXIR Europe Slack workspace. Invites go through us, and we add you to the ELIXIR Ireland private channel at the same time.

    Request a Slack invite

  5. Subscribe to the ELIXIR Weekly Brief

    ELIXIR's newsletter for members, every Monday: news, calls, events and requests from the Hub and Nodes.

    Subscribe Other ELIXIR newsletters

  6. Note your Hub contacts

    The ELIXIR Hub in Cambridge coordinates the groups. Each group has a Hub liaison you can email directly with questions about joining or taking part.

    See Hub contacts

Funding, exchanges and events

Talk to us first. Before applying for any ELIXIR call or joining a European project through ELIXIR, contact the Head of Node and Node Coordinator (Aedín Culhane, Gavin Farrell). They check fit with the Node's plans and support your application.

  • Commissioned Services: funded projects run by Nodes to fill gaps ELIXIR has identified.
  • Capacity building and knowledge exchange: staff exchanges between Nodes. Travel grant details are on the intranet.
  • Industry Knowledge Exchange Scheme: exchanges with companies.
  • European projects: ELIXIR leads and joins Horizon Europe consortia such as the European Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI).
  • Everything ELIXIR offers members: ELIXIR members page.

Two events worth knowing

  • ELIXIR All Hands: ELIXIR's annual meeting each June, with all Nodes, Platforms and Communities. Registration is on the intranet.
  • BioHackathon Europe: a week-long hackathon each November where teams work on tools, standards and data projects.

All ELIXIR events: elixir-europe.org/events.
