🔬 Welcome to the 𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗫𝗜𝗥 𝗜𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗕𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱, Dr @Therese Murphy!

Therese is a Lecturer in the School of Biological, Health and Sports Sciences at @Technological University Dublin (TU Dublin), and represents TU Dublin on our Management Board.

Her research focuses on the epigenomics of complex diseases, particularly depression and inflammatory disorders, combining DNA methylation profiling with machine learning for biomarker discovery. Recent work includes a methylome-wide association study of major depression in Nature Mental Health (2025).
