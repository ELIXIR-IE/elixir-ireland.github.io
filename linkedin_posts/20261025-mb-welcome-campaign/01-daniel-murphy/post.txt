🧬 Welcome to the 𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗫𝗜𝗥 𝗜𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗕𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱, Dr @Daniel Murphy!

Daniel is Assistant Professor in Computational Biology in the School of Biotechnology at @Dublin City University (DCU), and represents DCU on our Management Board.

His research spans genomics, transcriptomics, clinical bioinformatics and precision medicine. He previously helped define reference gene sets at the Wellcome Sanger Institute and EMBL-EBI, including work on Ensembl and the MANE transcript set for clinical genomics, before roles in clinical bioinformatics and in genomics industry.

📅 Daniel is also hosting VIBE 2026 at DCU Glasnevin on 3 December.
