## How to post
1. Paste `post.txt` into the LinkedIn composer.
2. **Tags:** LinkedIn can't take a pasted tag. Wherever the text says
   `@Name`, delete that `@Name`, type `@` plus the first few letters, and
   pick the person or organisation page from the dropdown. If a person isn't
   on LinkedIn, or doesn't show up, delete the `@` and leave their name as
   plain text.
3. Upload `linkedin-image.png` (1200×1200). Each folder also has the original
   16:9 welcome slide if you'd rather use that.
4. Day 1 only: `new-member-institutes-2026.webp` is the site's news-card
   image, an alternative if you'd rather let the link preview show.

## Before tagging, check
- Personal profiles aren't verified here. Make sure you pick the right person
  (common names: Daniel Murphy, Therese Murphy, John Ronan).
- Organisation pages: pick each university's official page.
- Day 1 text names RCSI, UCC, UCD, University of Galway and University of
  Limerick untagged. Tag them too if you want to (it adds reach but crowds
  the post).
- Expertise lines match the confirmed fact sheet
  (`_mb-welcome-slides/member-profiles-sources.md`). If you change anything
  there, change it here too.
