🦠 Welcome to the 𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗫𝗜𝗥 𝗜𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗕𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱, Dr @Francesca Bottacini!

Francesca is a Lecturer in the Department of Biological Sciences at @Munster Technological University (MTU), with a joint affiliation at APC Microbiome Ireland, University College Cork. She represents MTU on our Management Board.

Her research applies comparative genomics to beneficial gut bacteria, especially bifidobacteria: their genome diversity, how they colonise the infant gut, and their interactions with bacteriophages. Recent work includes genome-scale resources for the infant gut symbiont Bifidobacterium breve, published in Cell (2025).
