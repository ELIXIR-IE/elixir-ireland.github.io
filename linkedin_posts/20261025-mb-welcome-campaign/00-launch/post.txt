🤝 ELIXIR Ireland has doubled to 𝘁𝗲𝗻 𝗺𝗲𝗺𝗯𝗲𝗿 𝗶𝗻𝘀𝘁𝗶𝘁𝘂𝘁𝗲𝘀.

A warm welcome to @Dublin City University (DCU), @Munster Technological University (MTU), @South East Technological University (SETU), @Trinity College Dublin (TCD) and @Technological University Dublin (TU Dublin). They join RCSI, UCC, UCD, the University of Galway and the University of Limerick in the Irish Node of ELIXIR, Europe's life science data infrastructure.

Each new institute now has a representative on the 𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗫𝗜𝗥 𝗜𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗕𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱: @Daniel Murphy (DCU), @Francesca Bottacini (MTU), @John Ronan (SETU), @Máire Ní Leathlobhair (TCD) and @Therese Murphy (TU Dublin). We will introduce each of them here over the coming days.

🎥 We welcomed them at our 22 October onboarding session, an introduction to VIBE, ELIXIR Europe and ELIXIR Ireland. The recording is now online.
