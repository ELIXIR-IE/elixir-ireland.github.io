🧬 Welcome to the 𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗫𝗜𝗥 𝗜𝗿𝗲𝗹𝗮𝗻𝗱 𝗠𝗮𝗻𝗮𝗴𝗲𝗺𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗕𝗼𝗮𝗿𝗱, Dr @Máire Ní Leathlobhair!

Máire is an Assistant Professor in the School of Genetics and Microbiology at @Trinity College Dublin (TCD), and represents TCD on our Management Board.

Her research brings genomics and evolutionary biology to cancer, including rare and clonally transmissible cancers. Recent work includes the genomic landscape of adult testicular germ cell tumours in the 100,000 Genomes Project (Nature Communications, 2024).

Máire hosted VIBE 2025 at Trinity and, this October, the 𝗘𝗟𝗜𝗫𝗜𝗥 𝗙𝗲𝗱𝗲𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗱 𝗛𝘂𝗺𝗮𝗻 𝗗𝗮𝘁𝗮 𝗖𝗼𝗺𝗺𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝘆 meeting in Dublin.
