ELIXIR Federated Human Data Community meets in Dublin

Trinity College Dublin (TCD) hosted the 2026 ELIXIR Federated Human Data Community meeting on 8–9 October, with support from ELIXIR Ireland colleagues at the University of Limerick (UL) and the ELIXIR Hub. It took place in the same week as the final GDI General Assembly in Dublin.
