About the session

A one-hour introduction, from the ground up, to ELIXIR, Europe's life science data infrastructure, and to ELIXIR Ireland, the Irish Node. It covers what ELIXIR is, how the Node works across its member institutes, and the practical ways you and your group can get involved and benefit.

Who it is for

Everyone at an ELIXIR Ireland member institute is welcome, whatever your career stage or role:

  • PhD students and postdoctoral researchers
  • Lecturers, principal investigators and researchers
  • Digital research technical professionals, including research software engineers and bioinformaticians
  • Life science data managers and data stewards
  • Anyone with a general interest in life science data and research infrastructure

You do not need any prior knowledge of ELIXIR.

  • BioHackathon Europe, the annual hackathon event
  • Industry and SME days, and the Knowledge Exchange Scheme
  • Training and networking across the network
