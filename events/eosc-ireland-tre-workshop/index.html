About the workshop

This EOSC Ireland workshop sets out to identify the way forward towards a roadmap for sensitive data infrastructure in EOSC Ireland, with a focus on Trusted Research Environments (TREs) and Secure Processing Environments (SPEs).

The day combines talks with active discussion and planning sessions. It covers sensitive data access, login and authentication mechanisms (AAI via LS Login), and experience from the teams leading European TRE and SPE solutions. Key use cases and stakeholders from EOSC Ireland and across research domains are being invited to take part.
