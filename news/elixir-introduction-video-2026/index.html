ELIXIR Europe has released a new short video introducing Europe's life science data infrastructure, told by the people behind it.

12 October 2026

An introduction to ELIXIR: Europe's life science data infrastructure

In four and a half minutes, people from across ELIXIR explain how it connects data, resources and expertise across Europe to accelerate research and cross-border collaboration. It is a good starting point for anyone new to ELIXIR, and a handy one to share with colleagues and students.

ELIXIR Ireland is the Irish Node in that network, connecting life science researchers and services here to the wider European infrastructure. Find out more about ELIXIR Ireland and how to get involved.
