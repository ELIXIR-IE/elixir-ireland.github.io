EOSC Ireland Service Provider Readiness Survey

EOSC Ireland is mapping the national research service landscape.
If you run an operational research service in Ireland, in any research domain, we would like to hear about it.

8 September 2026

The EOSC Ireland project is designing Ireland's national node of the European Open Science Cloud, and it needs an accurate picture of the research services this country actually runs. The survey is open to every operational research service hosted or managed in Ireland: data repositories, curated databases, compute clusters, storage, analysis platforms, workflow and interoperability services, and training portals.

It takes about 15 minutes and closes on Monday 12 October 2026 at 17:00 (Irish Standard Time).

What is EOSC Ireland

EOSC Ireland will be the national connection point to the wider EOSC Federation, a network of national and thematic EOSC Nodes. It will not host or manage your service: providers keep their own infrastructure, access policies and quotas, and there is no cost to onboard.

Who should respond

Operational national or domain-specific research services hosted or managed in Ireland, in any research domain. If you host more than one very distinct, non-overlapping service, a separate response for each is ideal.

In scope

  • Data repositories and archives
  • Curated knowledgebases and thematic databases
  • General-purpose storage and data transfer
  • Compute, including HPC clusters
  • Scientific software, tools and analysis platforms
  • Interoperability and standards services
  • Training and e-learning portals

Out of scope

  • Off-the-shelf commercial tools
  • Small-scale individual research scripts
  • Personal project websites
  • Raw, unmanaged datasets
  • Physical scientific instrumentation

Not sure whether your service is in scope? It very likely is, or email gavin.farrell@ul.ie and ask.
