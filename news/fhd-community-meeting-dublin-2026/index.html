ELIXIR Federated Human Data Community meets in Dublin

Trinity College Dublin hosted the 2026 ELIXIR Federated Human Data Community meeting on 8–9 October, a joint effort between Trinity and the University of Limerick that brought Europe's human data community to Ireland.

13 October 2026

The ELIXIR Federated Human Data (FHD) Community held its 2026 meeting at Trinity Business School, Trinity College Dublin, on 8–9 October, with colleagues from across Europe attending in person and online. The Community works to build a federated ecosystem of interoperable services so that sensitive human data can be shared for research across borders while remaining under national governance.

Over two days, participants shared updates on projects across Europe and internationally, with open discussions on the Federated European Genome-phenome Archive (Federated EGA), compliance in light of the planned Genome European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (Genome EDIC), and funding opportunities.

Hosted by Trinity, supported by Limerick

The meeting was a collaboration between two ELIXIR Ireland member institutions, and both were essential to it.

Local host: Trinity College Dublin

Máire Ní Leathlobhair, Trinity's representative on the ELIXIR Ireland Management Board, was local host, welcoming the Community to Trinity Business School in the heart of Dublin.

Organisation: University of Limerick

The lab of Aedín Culhane, ELIXIR Ireland Head of Node, supported the organisation of the meeting, with Katie Cremin and colleagues looking after the planning and logistics that made the two days run smoothly.

Hosting in Dublin also made it easy for Irish researchers and infrastructure teams to take part in person, strengthening the national voice in a Community that shapes how human genomic and health data are shared across Europe. Our thanks to the FHD Community co-leads and to ELIXIR Europe for bringing the meeting to Ireland.

A big week for genomic data in Dublin

Participants at the final GDI General Assembly, held in Dublin in the same week. Photo: GDI.

The FHD meeting was timed alongside the final General Assembly of the European Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI) project, which also met in Dublin that week, during Ireland's Presidency of the Council of the European Union. The GDI General Assembly was organised by the GDI project. There, the 1+ Million Genomes (1+MG) infrastructure deployment pillar demonstrated national deployment and connectivity using Genome of Europe data, cancer use case data and synthetic data, and each GDI node country presented its progress towards connecting to the GDI user portal and its aims to March 2027.

Ireland takes part through GDI Ireland, co-led by RCSI and UCD with the University of Limerick and Maynooth University, with the Culhane lab at UL working on the deployment of the Irish node.

The week also saw the publication of the white paper Federated EGA in the European and Global Human Data Ecosystem, which sets out a vision for the Federated EGA and its role in the fast-developing European and global ecosystem for human genomic and health data.

ELIXIR Ireland is proud to have helped bring the Federated Human Data Community to Dublin, and looks forward to building on the week's conversations with partners across Ireland and Europe.
