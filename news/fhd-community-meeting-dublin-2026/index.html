ELIXIR Federated Human Data Community meets in Dublin

Trinity College Dublin hosted the 2026 ELIXIR Federated Human Data Community meeting on 8–9 October, bringing Europe's human data community to Ireland with Máire Ní Leathlobhair as local host.

13 October 2026

The ELIXIR Federated Human Data (FHD) Community held its 2026 meeting at Trinity Business School, Trinity College Dublin (TCD), on 8–9 October, with colleagues from across Europe attending in person and online. The Community works to build a federated ecosystem of interoperable services so that sensitive human data can be shared for research across borders while remaining under national governance.

Over two days, participants shared updates on projects across Europe and internationally, with open discussions on the Federated European Genome-phenome Archive (Federated EGA), compliance in light of the planned Genome European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (Genome EDIC), and funding opportunities.

Hosted at Trinity College Dublin, supported by ELIXIR Ireland

Local host: Máire Ní Leathlobhair, TCD

Máire Ní Leathlobhair, who recently joined the ELIXIR Ireland Management Board as TCD representative, was local host and led the meeting on the ground: securing the venue, managing the bookings and welcoming the Community to Dublin.

Máire worked on the organisation with ELIXIR Ireland colleagues at the University of Limerick (UL), in conjunction with the ELIXIR Hub. Katie Cremin and Tricia English, from the lab of ELIXIR Ireland Head of Node Aedín Culhane, provided coordination support to TCD, including the financial processing of bookings.

Hosting in Dublin also made it easy for Irish researchers and infrastructure teams to take part in person, strengthening the national voice in a Community that shapes how human genomic and health data are shared across Europe.

Special thanks

To the FHD Community co-leads, the ELIXIR Hub, and everyone at TCD and UL whose work made the meeting a success.

A big week for human data in Dublin

The week also saw the publication of the white paper Federated EGA in the European and Global Human Data Ecosystem, which sets out a vision for the Federated EGA and its role in the fast-developing European and global ecosystem for human genomic and health data.

The FHD meeting was timed alongside the final General Assembly of the European Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI) project, which also met in Dublin that week. Read more in our separate story: Final GDI General Assembly held in Dublin.

ELIXIR Ireland is proud to have helped bring the Federated Human Data Community to Dublin, and looks forward to building on the week's conversations with partners across Ireland and Europe.
