New ELIXIR onboarding videos developed through the PeoplePulse Commissioned Service

Three short videos introduce ELIXIR Nodes, the ELIXIR-GOBLET Train-the-Trainer programme, and ELIXIR's industry activities — through the perspectives of ELIXIR members.

11 August 2026

Three new onboarding videos, developed through the PeoplePulse Commissioned Service, introduce different parts of ELIXIR through the perspectives of ELIXIR members. They cover the activities of ELIXIR Nodes, the ELIXIR-GOBLET Train-the-Trainer programme, and ELIXIR's industry programme.

The videos are intended as an onboarding resource for newcomers to ELIXIR, helping explain how ELIXIR works from the inside and where to find further information.

ELIXIR Ireland spotlight: Dr Maria Doyle, ELIXIR-IE Training Coordinator at the University of Limerick, led the production of the Train-the-Trainer video as part of the previous PeoplePulse work programme activity. Congratulations, Maria, on this excellent contribution!
